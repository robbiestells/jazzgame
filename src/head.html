Scene 1, the practice room: listen to the hook and play it back three times. Scene 2, the club: the whole band kicks in, and if you earned it, the solo is yours.

Difficulty

Headphones help. Playing needs sound, so the music starts when you press the button. Bluetooth headphones add delay: use Sync audio first.

Settings

Bluetooth headphones usually need +150 to +300 ms. If the sound arrives before the notes reach the line, lower it; if after, raise it. The notes and your timing both move with this. Sync audio measures it for you.

Sync audio

Tap any pad, or press D F J K, in time with the clicks. Ignore the first few. Eight taps are enough.
